# Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

> Draft for counsel review (docs/plan/15-final-mile.md §3). Not a published
> policy until counsel has reviewed and approved it.

1. We store: your GitHub identity, your submitted scores, transcript
   hashes, and redacted transcripts you choose to upload.
2. We never receive your model API keys. The harness redacts key-shaped
   strings before signing; the upload contains hashes, not keys.
3. Redacted transcripts are public by design (they are the evidence).
   Redact before submitting; the `longrun redact` tool helps, but the
   responsibility is yours.
4. We use no tracking cookies. Analytics, if any, is self-hosted and
   aggregated.
5. Data deletion: scores are append-only and cannot be deleted (they are
   the public record), but your account and future submissions can be
   removed on request. Revoked scores stay visible as revoked.
