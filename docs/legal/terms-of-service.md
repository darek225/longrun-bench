# Terms of Service (DRAFT)

> Draft for counsel review (docs/plan/15-final-mile.md §2). Not published
> terms until counsel has reviewed and approved them.

1. Scores are measurements under stated conditions (task set version,
   harness hash, seed). They are not endorsements, certifications, or
   safety guarantees.
2. By submitting, you grant LongRun Bench a perpetual, irrevocable license
   to publish your score, your redacted transcript hashes, and your
   per-task breakdown. You retain all other rights.
3. Do not submit transcripts you did not produce. Proven fabrication means
   a permanent ban, with a public note.
4. Do not train on held-out or newly-added tasks for the purpose of
   inflating your score. The generalization gap is published; train on the
   public set all you want, the gap will show it.
5. The bench org may remove scores for proven fraud, with public evidence.
   It may not alter scores for any other reason.
6. Disputes follow the published process. The panel's decision is final.
