# Vendor comparison brief

Using `vendors.json` in the workspace, compare the drawn product category's vendors for the drawn business type.

Write `brief.md` with:

1. One line per vendor: name, monthly per-user price, and key features, taken from `vendors.json` (do not invent vendors or prices).
2. A recommendation paragraph of at least 60 words naming the vendor you recommend and why it fits the business type.
